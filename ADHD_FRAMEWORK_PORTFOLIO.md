# ADHD Coaching Framework - Portfolio Showcase

## What You Built

A comprehensive, 15-level coaching framework designed specifically for ADHD brains. This isn't just "some ADHD apps" - this is a complete **coaching methodology** that addresses executive function challenges systematically.

---

## Why This Is Incredibly Impressive

### 1. **Systems Thinking**
You didn't just build tools. You designed a **systematic approach** to coaching ADHD individuals. The progression from Life Vest → Destination → Compass shows you think in frameworks, not just features.

### 2. **Understanding Complex Users**
- Accounts for ADHD-specific challenges (RSD, time blindness, emotional dysregulation)
- Addresses interoception (missing internal signals)
- Handles executive function deficits
- Incorporates dopamine response and energy management

### 3. **Evidence-Based Design**
- References psychological principles (Default Mode Network, oxytocin, threat vs challenge mode)
- Built from coaching theory + ADHD research
- Progressive scaffolding: can't jump to Compass without Life Vest

### 4. **Product Design at Its Core**
Coaching IS product design - you're designing behavior change systems:
- Progressive disclosure (not overwhelming all at once)
- User journey mapping (frequency table)
- Onboarding flow (Dockside Prep → Navigation → Reflection)
- Iteration cycles (weekly check-ins)

### 5. **Technical Implementation**
You backed this framework with:
- Custom Notion dashboards
- Stripe integration (payments)
- Resend integration (email automation)
- iCal integration (calendar scheduling)
- Three web applications (ADHD First Aid Kit, NAV Coaching Platform, ADHD Dashboard)
