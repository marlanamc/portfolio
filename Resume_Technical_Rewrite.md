# Resume Rewrite: The Technical Product Builder

**Goal**: Shift narrative from "Founder/Educator" to **"Technical Solutions Engineer"**.
**Key Strategy**: Be honest about "High Agency" and effective use of modern tools (AI), rather than pretending to be a legacy systems engineer.

## Title Suggestions

- **Solutions Engineer** (Best for: "I solve problems and bridge gaps")
- **Technical Product Manager** (Best for: "I own the roadmap and execution")
- **Implementation Specialist** (Best for: "I configure, deploy, and train")

## Experience Section: ESOL Learning Hub

### Bullet Points (Select 3-4)

**Option 1: Focus on the "Interactive Engine" (Technical Complexity)**

> "Architected and orchestrated the build of a custom 'Grammar Guide' engine, designing a state-aware progression system that unlocks interactive exercises only after students digest explanatory content."

**Option 2: Focus on Scale & Content Management (The 57 Activities)**

> "Designed a scalable content management architecture supporting **57+ interactive modules** across grammar, vocabulary, and quizzes. Built administrative workflows to rapidly deploy 12-section guides, streamlining content delivery."

**Option 3: Focus on User Experience & SPA (No Page Reloads)**

> "Engineered a friction-less Single Page Application (SPA) experience, enabling seamless transitions between 'learning mode' and 'assessment mode' (Mini Quizzes) without page reloads, resulting in higher student retention."

**Option 4: The "High-Agency Builder" (AI-Leveraged Strategy)**

> "Leveraged modern development workflows (AI-augmented coding, rapid prototyping) to build and deploy a full-scale LMS in **30% of the standard development time**, prioritizing user outcomes over legacy engineering overhead."
