- Consistent content creation (hard for ADHD)
- Self-promotion (triggers RSD)
- Managing multiple channels (executive function drain)
- Dealing with rejection (many nos before yes)

### 2. **You Built The Product, Not The Business**
- ✅ Methodology: DONE

### 3. **ADHD Energy Challenges**
- Coaching is emotionally draining
- Requires consistency

- Marketing feels like rejection feedback loop

## The Honest Truth

**Building the product:** ✅ You did this

Marketing isn't your strength right now. That's OK. Many great products never scale because the founder doesn't enjoy/doesn't have energy for sales/marketing.

**BUT:** Your framework IS valuable. The question is: do you want to productize it differently, or focus on tech roles and use coaching as secondary/side work?
