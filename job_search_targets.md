## 💡 Application Strategy Tips

### **Resume Selection:**
- **Read the job description** - choose the resume that best matches the keywords and requirements
- **Default to EdTech resume** if unsure - it shows your teaching background well

### **Company Research:**
- **Check company size** - start with mid-sized companies (100-1000 employees) that value individual contributors
- **Look for Boston offices** - prioritize companies with local presence for easier interviews
- **Check Glassdoor reviews** - understand company culture and interview process

### **Application Volume:**
- **Start with 5 applications/week** - quality over quantity
- **Track everything** in your job application tracker
- **Follow up** after 1 week if no response
