## 🏥 Healthcare/Hospital Jobs

### Clinical Scheduling Administrators
- **Dana Farber** and other big hospitals are hiring "clinical scheduling administrators"
- Schedule patient appointments and coordinate appointments among different departments
- Basic white collar job that doesn't require a particular degree
- **Note:** Dana Farber's hiring process is very fast - one user reported their entire process from application to hire was 1 week
- **Also hiring:** Boston Children's Hospital
- **Warning:** These roles can be challenging:
  - You'll deal with many angry and scared people whose appointments might be much further out than they want
  - People may argue, threaten, or try to guilt you to get appointment slots that don't exist
  - You may be verbally abused by phone many times a day
  - High turnover is common
  - **However:** Hospital jobs have good benefits and strong unions backing the admin staff
  - Requires patience and compassion - remember patients are scared for good reasons

### MGH (Massachusetts General Hospital)
- Uses **Bullfinch Temps** for temporary opportunities
- Usually always hiring for temp work
- Since it's temp work, they don't care if you're overqualified and might leave once something better comes along
- Link: https://www.massgeneralbrigham.org/en/about/careers/temporary-opportunities

## 🏢 Temp Agencies & Staffing

### General Advice
- **Highly recommended approach** - Temp agencies can get you a job fast
- You can tell them where you want to end up and they can help steer you in that direction
- When you're down to the wire, agencies have genuine leads
- Many companies always post jobs but rarely hire - agencies have better resources and know which companies actually hire
- You can lay out qualifications for your field but also let the agency know you're available for anything right now

### Specific Agencies Mentioned
- **PSG Staffing** - One user got 2 jobs through them
- **Temp agencies** - Can help you get temp work that may lead to permanent positions
  - Example: Someone got a temp job at health services in Harvard Med, which led to their perfect job at the US-Japan program

## 🍽️ Restaurant & Food Service

### General Restaurant Jobs
- **Boston Chefs** website: https://www.bostonchefs.com/restaurant_jobs/
- You can narrow down options by role
- Fast hiring process possible:
  - Apply in the morning
  - Interview mid-day
  - Trial shift that night
  - Start two days later
- **Note:** Response times vary - some get back to you quickly, others take a month or more
- Apply to many places until one matches your time frame

### Dishwasher Positions
- One commenter suggested walking restaurant to restaurant to find dishwasher positions
- **Reality check:** This approach doesn't really work unless you're referred by someone
- Most places will tell you to apply online
- Employers get dozens of applications and won't stop to evaluate someone on the spot
- Dishwasher is actually the easiest role to fill, so competition may be high

### Catering
- **Catering agencies** hiring for server/bartender positions:
  - The Catered Affairs
  - Above & Beyond catering
  - At Your Service
  - East Meets West
  - Rita's Catering
  - Event Temps
- Right now is busy season - they need people
- Can get at least 40 hours/week
- Lowest pay is $22/hr with tips from some agencies
- One user offered to help get a restaurant job in the city (DM them)

## 💼 General Job Search Strategy

### Resume & Application Tips
- **Consider minimizing previous work experience** if it's in a different field
- Recruiters may not hire someone for a job they think you'll leave as soon as you find an opening in your chosen field
- For entry-level jobs, consider leaving off overqualified experience
- **Specifying your field** would be helpful - more likely to find a match

### Job Search Resources
- **Mass Hire Downtown** - has regular job fairs and good career counselors
- Ask their career counselors to look at your resume
- Attend their job fairs
- **Operation ABLE** - if you're over 45, they may be able to help with resumes & placements

### What to Avoid
- **State and federal employment** - never quick (state is under a hiring freeze)
- **Gig work** (Fiverr, Uber, etc.) - you probably already know about these
- Walking into places unannounced - generally doesn't work anymore unless you have a referral

---

## ⚠️ Important Considerations

### About Overqualification
- Employers don't want to invest time and money training someone who's obviously keeping one foot out the door
- Consider how you present your experience when applying outside your field

### Reality Check
- Many companies post jobs but rarely actually hire
- The job market is tough - many people are in the same situation
- Some positions have very limited hours (e.g., Whole Foods only guarantees 4 hours/week)

### Support
- Multiple commenters mentioned being in the same boat
- The process can feel dehumanizing
- Keep applying and don't give up
