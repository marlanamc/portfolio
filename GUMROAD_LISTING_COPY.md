### **The Framework Structure**

**🦺 Dockside Prep (Foundation)**
- Life Vest: Self-compassion mantras to interrupt shame spirals
- Fuel Up: Nervous system regulation check-ins
- North Star: Core values and internal motivation

**🧭 Navigation Tools**
- Destination: Main goal in third-person framing
- Lighthouse: Long-term vision (6 months, 1 year, 5 years)
- Anchor: Weekly grounding question
- Compass: 1-3 concrete weekly priorities
- Sails/Oars: Energy mapping and management

**🌩️ Hazard Recognition**
- Storms: Big challenges that knock you off course
- Drift/Sirens: Sneaky distractions and avoidance patterns

**🛟 Support Systems**
- Lifeboat: Executive function support (PARA method, brain dumps)
- Buoy: Weekly check-in ritual
- First Mate: Accountability partner/system
- Logbook: Progress reflection method
- Starlight: Wins and gratitude recognition
