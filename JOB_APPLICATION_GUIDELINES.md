# Job Application Guidelines

## Instructions for Creating Resumes and Cover Letters

When I provide you with a job posting, please help me create a tailored resume and cover letter following these guidelines:

### Cover Letter Format
- **Style**: Concise, conversational tone (4-5 paragraphs maximum)
- **Format**: Match the format used in `job_applications/Marlana_Creed_BPS_IT_Project_Manager_Cover_Letter.html`
  - Centered header with name, contact info, and blue border
  - Right-aligned date
  - Short, punchy paragraphs with `<div class="content">` tags
  - Closing with "Sincerely" signature
- **Content**: Focus on 2-3 key experiences that directly relate to the role
- **Writing style**: 
  - NO em-dashes
  - Use apostrophes: "I'm" not "I am"
  - Be conversational, not formal
  - Keep it honest - don't exaggerate my HKS research work
  - Don't mention ADHD coaching or Barcelona Restaurant Group unless directly relevant
  - Focus on what matters to me: systems building, education, accessibility, community work

### Resume Format
- **Style**: Match the format used in `Marlana_Creed_EdTech_resume_2025.html`
  - Centered header with name, title, contact info, and blue border
  - Sections with blue underline borders
  - Clean spacing, muted colors for dates/company names
  - Use `<strong>` tags for job titles and section headers
- **Sections**:
  - Summary (2-3 sentences max)
  - Skills (concise bullet points or short lines)
  - Experience (3-4 bullet points per role)
  - Education
- **Length**: Must fit on 1 page when printed
- **Content**: 
  - Remove ADHD coaching and Barcelona Restaurant Group references unless directly relevant
  - Include relevant tech skills and methodologies
  - Focus on impact and outcomes
  - Be truthful about HKS work - focus on methods/analysis, not claims about findings

### Key Experiences to Highlight (when relevant)
1. **ESOL Instruction**: Teaching adult learners, building educational tools, community work
2. **Systems Building**: Custom LMS, educational applications, process improvement
3. **Data Analytics**: Tableau, SQL, statistical analysis, data visualization
4. **Research Methods**: HKS research experience (analysis methods, not findings)
5. **Full-stack Development**: Python, Flask, React, Firebase, SQL
6. **Community Engagement**: Working with underserved populations, immigrant communities

### File Naming
- Resume: `job_applications/Marlana_Creed_[CompanyName]_Resume.html`
- Cover Letter: `job_applications/Marlana_Creed_[CompanyName]_Cover_Letter.html`

### Workflow
1. Read the job posting carefully
2. Identify 2-3 most relevant experiences for the cover letter
3. Tailor resume sections to highlight relevant skills
4. Ensure both documents are 1 page when printed
5. Use the date provided by me in the conversation

### What NOT to Include
- ADHD coaching (unless directly relevant to role)
- Barcelona Restaurant Group (unless directly relevant to role)
- Exaggerated claims about research findings
- Em-dashes
- Overly formal language
- Dense paragraphs
