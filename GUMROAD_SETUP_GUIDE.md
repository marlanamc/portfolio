# Gumroad Setup Guide: ADHD Coaching Framework

## Quick Setup Steps

### 1. Create Account (5 minutes)
- Go to gumroad.com
- Click "Sign Up" (top right)
- Use your Gmail (marlana.creed@gmail.com)
- Verify email

### 2. Create Product (10 minutes)
- Click "Products" → "New Product"
- Upload your Notion template (or PDF)
- Add product details (see below)

### 3. Set Price (2 minutes)
- Suggested: $39 (starter), $59 (with bonuses)
- Gumroad takes 10% + payment processing fee (~3%)

### 4. Add Screenshots (5 minutes)
- Take 3-4 screenshots of your framework
- Show structure, examples, resource guides
- Add captions explaining what they show

### 5. Publish (1 minute)
- Click "Publish"
- Get your product link
- Share it!

## What to Upload

### Option A: Notion Template (BEST)
**File type:** Notion template URL or exported .zip
**What:** Your levels.md content formatted as Notion workspace

**How to share Notion template:**
1. Open your levels Notion page
2. Click "Share" (top right)
3. Toggle "Share to web"
4. Click "Copy link"
5. Add to Gumroad product file

### Option B: PDF Bundle
**File type:** PDF
**What:** Exported resource guides

**If you don't have Notion template ready:**
- Export your levels.md as PDF
- Can upgrade to Notion template later
